Afterglow 2.1.1
Windows studio, synced lyrics and MSI setup

Install Afterglow-2.1.1-x64.msi. Existing settings and pairing are preserved.
New users get a browser setup guide. The extension still needs Developer mode
and Load unpacked approval in Chrome or Edge.

Enable synced lyrics in Lyrics to show them beside the cover on the desktop.
Each phrase now retains its own visual while moving and changing emphasis.
This avoids overlapping panel fades, brightness dips and duplicate glyphs.
Seeking resumes from the displayed positions, and rapid advances do not extend
departing phrases indefinitely. Small late playback reports do not bounce the
lyrics backward. Long phrases wrap fully.

Wallpaper Studio offers Ambient, Gallery, Immersive and Backdrop. Appearance,
Playback and Connection remember your choices. Song fades use Windows GPU
composition; static PNG exports preserve full-resolution art and text.

See INSTALL.md, LYRICS.md, BUILD.md and VERIFICATION.md for setup, privacy,
build instructions and the limits of the checks performed.
