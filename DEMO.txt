Afterglow 1.4 - GPU desktop transitions
October 7, 2026

The Desktop executable and downloads are updated. Open the existing Afterglow shortcut. Your existing settings, pairing and original-wallpaper backup are retained.

The desktop now uses Windows GPU composition to blend two original-resolution images. Windows animates opacity at compositor cadence, with a gentler two-second fade. Images are shared across monitors. Rapid skips continue from a GPU snapshot of the held blend. The final PNG encodes alongside the fade, reducing the initial wait.

Click Test desktop fade to preview the actual desktop layer without skipping your music. The studio hides briefly, blends to a sample and back, then returns.

The existing YouTube Music extension is unchanged at 1.1.0. No extension reinstall or re-pairing is needed. Follow the playing song must be enabled for automatic wallpaper changes.

Verification: 40 integration/rendering checks, 10 extension checks and 15 native transition checks passed. The composed-desktop probe found zero color errors or backward jumps during concurrent 4K rendering. The test restored the wallpaper afterward. See VERIFICATION.md for measurements and their limits.

A Direct3D 11 hardware graphics device and enabled Windows interface animations are required for GPU fades. If composition is unavailable, Afterglow applies the finished PNG directly.
